About G22

G22 is a Filipino girl group formed in 2022 by Cornerstone Entertainment. Tagged as "P-pop's Female Alphas", the group is composed of three members: AJ, Alfea, and Jaz.

Members

AJ is the leader of the group, known for her powerful vocals and stage presence.

Alfea is the visual of the group, recognized for her stunning looks and dance skills.

Jaz is the vocalist of the group, celebrated for her lyrical prowess and unique style.

Their latest release!

The Dissection of Eve is the debut studio album by the Filipino girl group G22. It was released on November 22, 2025, by Cornerstone Entertainment. It contains eight tracks, two of which were released as singles, "Pa-Pa-Pa-Palaban" and "Filipina Queen". It was produced by Alfea Marie Gonzalez, Angel Jeremae Yape, Carlo Oraya, Jem Florendo, Michael Negapatan, Pauline Lauron, and Theo Martel Alpas.
